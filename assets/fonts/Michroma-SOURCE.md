# Michroma

`Michroma-Regular.ttf` and `Michroma-OFL.txt` were obtained from the [Google Fonts Michroma directory](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/michroma) on 2026-09-07. The font is embedded for the reference-based screen headings. Its SIL Open Font License and copyright notice are preserved in `Michroma-OFL.txt`.
